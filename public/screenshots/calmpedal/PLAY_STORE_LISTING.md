# CalmPedal — Google Play listing

> Working launch brief, 2026-08-14. Google Play only. Source of truth: the
> CalmPedal product strategy, marketing plan, market research, execution plan,
> and shipped mobile scope.

## Positioning

**Category hypothesis:** the sleep-aware cycling coach

**Audience:** committed, time-poor recreational cyclists who ride 2–5 times a
week and want one clear decision when sleep, fatigue, training load, and real
life disagree.

**Core job:** turn last night's sleep into today's ride call, then use today's
ride to plan for tonight.

**Lead promise:** **Know what today can take.**

**Narrative concept:** **The two-way ride**

```text
last night's sleep → today's ride call → tonight's plan
        ↑                                  ↓
        └──── CalmPedal learns the loop ───┘
```

This is deliberately narrower than “AI cycling coach” and more useful than a
generic recovery score. The listing should sell a calm decision, not more data.

```

### Short description — 65/80 characters

Turn last night's sleep into today's ride, then plan for tonight.
```

### Full description

```text
CalmPedal is a sleep-aware cycling coach that turns last night's sleep and recent training load into one clear ride call for today, then helps you plan for tonight.

Stop translating charts into a guess. CalmPedal brings ride history, sleep, HRV, resting heart rate and your own check-in together so you can see what today can take — and why.

A CLEAR DAILY RIDE CALL

See Rest, Recover, Easy or Train, with a recommended zone, duration, power range, latest finish time and confidence. When sleep or training load changes the call, CalmPedal shows the factors behind it.

PLAN THE RETURN LEG

Your ride does not end when you stop pedaling. Use the evening ride planner to explore timing and duration, see a latest finish time, and build a realistic wind-down and bedtime plan.

LEARN YOUR OWN PATTERN

Pair rides with the sleep before and after them. Over time, CalmPedal shows how your ride timing and intensity relate to your own sleep pattern, with visible confidence when the data is still thin.

ATLAS, GROUNDED IN YOUR DATA

Choose focused questions such as “What should I ride today?”, “Why did that change?”, “Can I ride tonight?” and “How is my sleep trending?” Atlas answers on-device from the same factors used in your daily recommendation.

USE THE DATA YOU ALREADY HAVE

Connect Health Connect to import compatible sleep sessions, exercise sessions, distance, elevation gained, power, HRV and resting heart rate. You can also log or correct rides and sleep manually. Availability and data completeness depend on your Android version, device and connected data source.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN

CalmPedal is local-first. Your raw health data stays on your device. You control Health Connect permissions, can export your data, and can delete it from the app.

BUILT FOR REAL RIDERS

• Adapt today's ride after a poor night
• Compare an evening ride with your usual bedtime
• Understand why a training recommendation changed
• Track paired sleep and cycling patterns
• Use manual logging when Health Connect is unavailable

CalmPedal is designed for adult recreational cyclists and general fitness and wellness. It is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any medical condition. Consult a healthcare professional for medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. Do not use CalmPedal as a substitute for road safety judgment or professional coaching.

CalmPedal is free to download. Some personalized prescriptions, scenario planning and long-term insights require an optional CalmPedal Pro subscription. Pricing and trial availability are shown in the app and may vary by region.
```

### Visual direction

- Keep the existing dark navy and cyan system; it feels like pre-dawn and
  blue-hour riding without resorting to generic wellness pastels.
- Use one continuous cyan route line from panels 2 to 3 as the only cross-screen
  composition. It becomes a sleep arc at the seam. Text and key UI remain wholly
  inside each panel.
- Make panel 3 and panel 7 AMOLED-black contrast beats.
- Keep taglines under roughly 20% of each image and prioritize legible real UI in
  the first three panels.
- Use the cadence ring as the recurring “ride” shape and the sleep arc as the
  recurring “night” shape. By panel 8 they become one closed loop.
- Do not use cycling lifestyle photography inside phone screenshots. If a photo
  is later tested, reserve it for the feature graphic or a separate store
  experiment.

### Health Connect permission declarations

Use the wording below when Play Console asks why CalmPedal needs each Android
Health permission. Keep every answer tied to a visible user benefit. Do not say
that CalmPedal diagnoses health, continuously monitors the user, or needs every
available Health Connect record.

Before submission, compare this table with the **merged release manifest** from
the uploaded AAB. Declare only permissions that are present in that manifest and
used by the shipped app. The current product scope supports read access only;
do not request the corresponding `WRITE_*` permissions unless CalmPedal gains a
real user-facing Health Connect export feature.

| Permission | Play Console description |
| --- | --- |
| `android.permission.health.READ_SLEEP` | CalmPedal reads sleep sessions, including sleep timing, duration and available stages, so the user can see how the previous night's sleep affects today's cycling recommendation. It also pairs a ride with the sleep before and after it to show the user's own sleep-and-riding patterns over time. This data is used for the user's daily ride call, sleep trends and explanations inside CalmPedal. |
| `android.permission.health.READ_EXERCISE` | CalmPedal reads cycling exercise sessions, including their start time, end time, duration and exercise type, to build the user's ride history and recent training load. This lets CalmPedal recommend an appropriate ride for today, explain why that recommendation changed and compare a planned evening ride with the user's bedtime. |
| `android.permission.health.READ_DISTANCE` | CalmPedal reads distance recorded for cycling sessions to complete the user's ride history and add training context to daily recommendations and personal ride-and-sleep insights. Distance is shown back to the user and is used only when evaluating their own rides. |
| `android.permission.health.READ_ELEVATION_GAINED` | CalmPedal reads elevation gained for cycling sessions to reflect the climbing demand of the user's rides. Elevation adds context to recent training load, daily ride recommendations and explanations of why a ride may have been more demanding than distance or duration alone suggests. The app remains usable when elevation data is unavailable. |
| `android.permission.health.READ_POWER` | CalmPedal reads available cycling power measurements to understand the intensity of the user's recorded rides. This supports personalized power ranges, recent-load calculations and explanations of whether today's suggested ride should be Rest, Recover, Easy or Train. The app remains usable when power data is unavailable. |
| `android.permission.health.READ_HEART_RATE_VARIABILITY` | CalmPedal reads available heart-rate-variability measurements as one recovery signal alongside sleep, recent rides and the user's check-in. It uses HRV to personalize and explain today's cycling recommendation and to show recovery trends. HRV is not used to diagnose or treat a medical condition. |
| `android.permission.health.READ_RESTING_HEART_RATE` | CalmPedal reads available resting-heart-rate measurements to compare the user's recent value with their own baseline. This adds recovery context to the daily ride call and helps explain why a recommendation changed. Resting heart rate is not used for diagnosis, treatment or emergency monitoring. |

#### Conditional permissions — declare only if shipped

These permissions are broader than a single record type and need especially
specific justification. Remove them from the manifest and declaration if the
release does not actually use the corresponding behavior.

| Permission | When it is justified | Play Console description |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `android.permission.health.READ_HEALTH_DATA_HISTORY` | Only if CalmPedal reads Health Connect records older than the platform's normal history window for its visible long-term pattern feature. | CalmPedal reads older Health Connect history only after the user connects the data source so it can build the long-term ride-and-sleep timeline shown in the Stats and personal-response screens. Historical sleep, cycling, distance, elevation, power, HRV and resting-heart-rate records are used to compare the user's own patterns over time. The app does not use this access for advertising or unrelated profiling. |
| `android.permission.health.READ_HEALTH_DATA_IN_BACKGROUND` | Only if a released background sync updates CalmPedal without the user opening the app. It is not justified for foreground refresh or app-open sync. | CalmPedal reads the health data types the user approved during scheduled background sync so the daily ride call and sleep-and-ride timeline are current when the user opens the app. Background access is limited to the same sleep, cycling, distance, elevation, power, HRV and resting-heart-rate features available in the foreground, and the user can disable access in Health Connect. |

Do **not** declare exercise routes, precise location, body sensors, heart rate,
calories, speed, cadence or other health types merely because a connected
device may have recorded them. CalmPedal should request each separate data type
only when the release reads it and exposes a corresponding user-facing benefit.
Health Connect maps cycling cadence to the exercise permission, but route access
has its own restricted flow and is not part of the current CalmPedal scope.

### Health apps declaration answers

| Question | CalmPedal answer |
| --- | --- |
| Health features | Select **Activity and Fitness** and **Sleep Management**. |
| Medical device | **No.** CalmPedal is a general fitness and wellness app. Keep the non-medical disclaimer in the full description. |
| External hardware | No CalmPedal-specific hardware is required. Imported data depends on Health Connect-compatible apps and devices; manual logging remains available. |
| Purpose of access | Fitness coaching, personal activity and sleep tracking, recovery context and user-visible ride planning. |
| Sharing or sale | Do not claim “none” until the Data safety form has been reconciled with RevenueCat, diagnostics and every production SDK. Health Connect data must not be sold or used for advertising. |
| User control | Users choose Health Connect permissions, can revoke them in Health Connect, can use manual logging, and can export or delete CalmPedal data from the app. |

`INTERNET`, `ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE`, billing and similar ordinary Android
permissions do not receive Health Connect data-type justifications. If Play
Console opens a separate high-risk Permissions Declaration Form after the AAB
is uploaded, answer it from the final merged manifest rather than copying these
health descriptions into unrelated permission fields.

Before any testing-track release, the privacy policy must be live, public,
non-geofenced, and linked both in Play Console and the app. Data safety answers
must match the implemented Health Connect, RevenueCat, diagnostics, export, and
deletion behavior. The health declaration is required even on testing tracks.

## Launch experiments

Do not test title, screenshots, price, and paywall at the same time.

1. **Screenshot story:** outcome-first sequence above vs. mechanism-first with
   “Your sleep changes the ride” in panel 1.
2. **Short description:** the bidirectional promise vs. “One clear cycling call
   from sleep, training load and real life today.”
3. **Feature graphic:** abstract cadence-to-sleep loop vs. restrained blue-hour
   cycling photography.

Run one variable at a time. Use the default listing first; add a sleep/recovery
custom store listing only when the acquisition page and shipped onboarding have
the same message. Judge winners by connected data, first confident prescription,
trial start, and retained sleep–ride loops—not store conversion alone.

## Policy and asset QA

- App name: maximum 30 characters.
- Short description: maximum 80 characters; no CTA, rankings, awards, price, or
  keyword stuffing.
- Full description: maximum 4,000 characters; natural prose, accurate paid
  feature notice, and non-medical disclaimer.
- Play icon: 512 × 512, 32-bit PNG with alpha, maximum 1 MB.
- Feature graphic: exactly 1024 × 500, JPEG or 24-bit PNG, no alpha.
- Phone screenshots: upload all eight; use 1080 × 1920 portrait, JPEG or 24-bit
  PNG with no alpha.
- Keep the first three screenshots focused on actual in-app experience.
- No fabricated social proof, rankings, awards, price claims, outcome guarantees,
  unsupported integrations, or “download now” language.
